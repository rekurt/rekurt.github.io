# Depth: what happens when liquidity disappears

Remove the nearest bids and asks in the example below. The spread widens from 2 to 6, even though both sides still hold the same total volume. A balanced book can have very little liquidity near the current price.

The two charts use **@rekurt/depth 0.1.0**. Prices and volumes are made up; the left chart stays fixed while you change the right one.
